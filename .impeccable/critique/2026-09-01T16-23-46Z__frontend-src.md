#### Overall Impression
SharkRecovery delivers an authoritative financial operations dashboard that balances high information density with immediate clarity. The 6-node agent telemetry pipeline and Indian banking error taxonomy provide instant credibility for fintech operators.

#### What's Working
1. **Authentic Domain Grounding**: Native Indian numbering format, Razorpay failure taxonomy (UPI limit, 3DS timeout, SBI 503), Twilio WhatsApp templates, and paise math.
